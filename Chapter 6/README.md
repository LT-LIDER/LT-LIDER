
# Chapter 6

**Capsule: Reviewing and discussing key concepts in large language models for translation**

Author(s) name and affiliation: Nora Aranberri, University of the Basque Country (UPV/EHU)

Expected study time: Flexible, depending on the number of questions selected and the extent of classroom discussion.

Language of instruction: Any language. Materials are provided in English but can be translated or adapted as needed. Classroom discussions can take place in the learners' preferred language.

## Learning outcomes (linked to the LT-LiDER skills map)

This capsule contributes to the development of the following skills:

- Know LLM and NMT limitations and capabilities.
- Understand Transformers.
- Understand word embeddings.
- Know prompt design.

Through concept review and discussion, learners are encouraged to consolidate their understanding of how LLMs work and reflect on the implications of their capabilities and limitations for translation.

## Description

This activity provides a set of multiple-choice questions based on Chapter 6, *Key Concepts in Large Language Models for Translation*, designed to review key concepts related to large language models (LLMs) and encourage discussion of their implications for translation.

The questions are intended as illustrative teaching resources rather than as a comprehensive review of the chapter or a formal assessment. Teachers are encouraged to select, modify, add or omit questions, answer options and discussion suggestions according to their teaching objectives and the needs of their learners.

## Suggested use

The questions can be used as a warm-up activity, to consolidate concepts after reading the chapter, or as a starting point for classroom discussion.

Teachers can select the questions most relevant to their session, invite learners to choose an answer, and use the accompanying explanations and discussion suggestions to explore selected concepts in greater depth.

The questions can be presented directly in the classroom or incorporated into an interactive quiz platform such as Kahoot. The use of Kahoot is entirely optional.

## Prerequisites

Prior reading of Chapter 6 is recommended but not essential. No programming skills are required.
